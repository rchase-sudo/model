05 · Construction · Phase 1

Clear and protect.

Trees inside the work area come down. Silt fence goes up around the disturbance to keep soil on site, and a stone construction entrance keeps mud off Route 73.

  • Silt fence around the limit of disturbance
  • Stone construction entrance at the curb cut
  • Woods beyond the property line stay
06 · Construction · Phase 2

Grade and connect.

The site is graded to its design elevations, and utility services are brought in from Route 73 before anything goes on top of them.

  • Water service
  • Sanitary sewer
  • Storm drainage
  • Electric

Utility routing shown is illustrative.

07 · Construction · Phase 3

Build and pave.

The building goes up — structure first, then walls and roof. The parking lot and loop drive are paved, replacing the construction entrance.

08 · Construction · Phase 4

Finish the ground.

Sidewalks, striping, lawn, and landscape trees go in. Once the ground is stabilized, the silt fence comes down.

09 · Operation

Opening day.

Customers turn in at the single Route 73 entrance, park or follow the loop drive, and exit the same way. Through traffic on Route 73 keeps moving past the site.
