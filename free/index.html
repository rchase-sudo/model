Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The layout plan, concept plan and 3D rendering cost nothing. No card, no contract. I do it because a lot of agents who see their listing built out want the full package for it, or for their next one.

What's the catch?

There isn't one. If you never buy anything, you keep the plan and the rendering and use them on your listing.

Can I put it on the MLS and my flyers?

Yes. That's what it's for. Post it, print it, send it to developers.

How is the plan made?

From the county parcel record, public LiDAR elevation data and the setbacks and permitted uses in your town's zoning code. No site visit or survey needed. If you have a survey, send it and I'll use it.

Is this an engineered plan?

No. It's a preliminary, conceptual plan to show buyers what fits. It is not a stamped engineering or permit set.

What's in the paid package?

Everything in the free plan plus a due diligence report: zoning, permitting roadmap and a pro forma. $949, delivered within 7 days and charged only when it's delivered, with a 6-month guarantee. Add-ons include a full civil plan set and an interactive property website.
