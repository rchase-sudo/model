02Services

Company prepares a preliminary design and due diligence package for the property identified by Client (the "Package"). The Listing Package includes a preliminary site plan, a 3D rendering and due diligence materials. Client may add:

  • Full plan set: a preliminary civil design for the proposed development (grading, drainage and stormwater, water, sewer and utility layout).
  • Property website: an interactive website presenting the plan, rendering and Package for the listing.

All deliverables, including the plan set, are conceptual and preliminary. They are not construction documents, stamped engineering drawings, legal surveys or permit documents.

The guarantee does not reduce or refund the Price.
