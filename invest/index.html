SHEET 02 · THE PROBLEM

Buyers don’t buy dirt. They buy what can be built on it.

The typical land listing

  • Photos of an empty lot and a plat map
  • Buyers guess at layout, zoning and numbers
  • A concept from an engineering firm costs thousands and takes weeks
  • Days on market climb while developers pass

A Forma listing

  • A conceptual site plan on current zoning and setbacks
  • A 3D rendering buyers can picture
  • A due diligence summary: costs and returns
  • Delivered in 7 days for a flat $949

Agent pays; the package is a marketing tool, not a stamped plan. Not for permitting or construction.

SHEET 11 · THE HOLES

What an investor should worry about.

Tiny revenue base

$1,230 from 4 clients. Zero sales at today’s price.

One part-time founder

Runs Forma alongside a day job. No team.

No measured CAC

Only cold calling has run. Ads and email are unproven.

Leads go cold

Strong calls, weak follow-up. No system closes after the call.

No outcome proof

No tracked data yet that packages sell listings faster.

Thin infrastructure

Framer subdomain, Gmail sender, pipeline still in build.

Offer liabilities

Payment plans can default; the guarantee owes free work.

Copyable

AI tools lower the bar for firms and rivals to do the same.
