02Services

Company prepares a preliminary design and due diligence package for the site and use identified by Client (the "Package"). The Package includes five (5) preliminary site layouts for that use, a CAD file (DWG) for each layout, a 3D rendering of one layout selected by Client, and a due diligence package (zoning, permitting roadmap and a pro forma). Client may add:

  • Preliminary plan set: a preliminary civil design for the proposed development (grading, drainage and stormwater, water, sewer and utility layout).
  • Project website: an interactive website presenting the layouts, rendering and Package.

All deliverables, including the plan set, are conceptual and preliminary. They are not construction documents, stamped or sealed engineering drawings, legal surveys or permit documents.
