Questions firms ask

What do I get for $949?

For one site and the use your client wants: 5 different concept layouts, a DWG for each, a photoreal 3D rendering of the layout your client picks, a due diligence package (zoning, permitting roadmap and a pro forma) and one revision round.

Where does this fit in a job?

After you've signed. It covers the concept and preliminary layout phase, so your client settles on a design before your team starts engineering it.

What CAD format do you deliver?

DWG, one per layout, drawn to scale on the parcel so your team can open it and keep working. Tell me your layer standards and I'll follow them.

Is it really white-label?

Yes. Nothing carries the Forma name. Send your logo and title block and I'll use them, or take it unbranded and drop it into your own sheets.

Will you contact my client?

No. I work only with you, and I never contact or take work directly from your clients.

What do you need from me?

The address and the use. A survey, the client's program or markups all help, so send whatever you have.

When am I charged?

Only when the package is delivered. You save a card on Stripe's secure checkout when you order, and nothing is charged that day.

Is anything stamped?

No. Everything I deliver is preliminary and conceptual. Your firm does the engineering and seals the work.

Sending several jobs a month?

Ask a question or book a call and we'll set up a standing arrangement for your firm.

How fast is it?

Within 7 days of your order. Tight deadline? Book a call and ask.
