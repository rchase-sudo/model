05 · Construction · Phase 1

Clear and protect.

Trees inside the work area come down. Silt fence goes up around the disturbance to keep soil out of the ravine, and a stone construction entrance keeps mud off the existing drive. The small shed (E2) and the old drive (P2) are removed.

  • Silt fence around the limit of disturbance
  • Stone construction entrance at the loop drive
  • Ravine, wetland and the rest of the woods stay
06 · Construction · Phase 2

Grade and connect.

The pad is graded and the stormwater pond is dug. Catch basins in the drive aisles pipe runoff to the pond, and water, sewer and electric come in from McMullen Booth Rd along the existing drive.

  • Water service
  • Sanitary sewer
  • Catch basins piped to the stormwater pond
  • Electric

Utility routing shown is illustrative.

07 · Construction · Phase 3

Build and pave.

The building goes up, structure first, then walls and roof. The drive aisles and parking are paved and tied into the existing loop drive.

08 · Construction · Phase 4

Finish the ground.

Walks, curbs, striping and landscaped islands go in, and the pond is filled and planted. Once the ground is stabilized, the silt fence comes down.

09 · Operation

Opening day.

Patients turn in from McMullen Booth Rd and follow the existing drive past the existing building to the new lot. Seventy-five spaces wrap the building, so every visit ends a short walk from the door.
