Lot 6, Pocahontas Trail, start to finish.

03 · The proposal

Two buildings, one entrance.

05 · Construction · Phase 1

Clear and protect.

Trees inside the work area come down. Silt fence goes up around the disturbance to keep soil on site, and a stone construction entrance keeps mud off US 60.

  • Silt fence around the limit of disturbance
  • Stone construction entrance at the curb cut
  • Wetlands and the rest of the woods stay
06 · Construction · Phase 2

Grade and connect.

The pad is graded and the stormwater ponds are dug. Catch basins at the low points of the lot pipe runoff to the ponds, and utilities come in from US 60. A single water and sewer tap serves both buildings.

  • Water service
  • Sanitary sewer
  • Catch basins piped to two stormwater ponds
  • Electric

Utility routing shown is illustrative.

07 · Construction · Phase 3

Build and pave.

Both buildings go up, structure first, then walls and roof. The loop drive and parking are paved, replacing the construction entrance.

08 · Construction · Phase 4

Finish the ground.

Walks, curbs, striping and landscaped islands go in, and the ponds are filled and planted. Once the ground is stabilized, the silt fence comes down.

09 · Operation

Opening day.

Customers turn in at the single US 60 entrance. Wash customers follow the loop into the bays; laundromat customers park steps from the door. Everyone leaves the way they came, and through traffic on US 60 keeps moving.
